Five Reasons Entrepreneurs Think This Won’t Work for Them

  1. 01

    “I don’t know who to pitch or what to say.”

    Decision makers book the speaker who clearly fits their audience, and most pitches never show why the person is a good fit. Instead, research the person who decides, learn one thing their audience needs, and open your pitch with their need, not about you and what you offer. That’s how a pitch for one of our clients, a criminal defense attorney, got a public high school to write back asking to meet.

  2. 02

    “I can just do this with AI.”

    AI works from whatever you give it, so without direction you get a generic list and a pitch that sounds like everyone else’s.   3. 03

    “I don’t have the time.”

    Getting booked comes from a small amount of work done every week, so give it 2 hours. One person who downloaded my free Speaker’s Vault pitched 35 organizations on her own within one week.

  4. 04

    “My list is small, and I’m not established enough.”

    Organizers book someone who can help their audience, and a small list is where everyone starts. Set up your lead magnet and welcome emails before your next talk, so every room adds people to your list.   5. 05

    “Will this actually pay off?”

    A room is worth the people in it: future clients, referral partners and the organizer who invites you back. Run the math on your own offer. One room a month with one client per room, at a $2,000 offer, is $24,000 a year (an example, so plug in your own price).
